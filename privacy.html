Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

Kamal Sons Pharmacy respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared and protected when you visit our website, contact us, submit an order request or arrange delivery.

1. Information We May Receive

When you contact us or submit an order request, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your name.
  • Your WhatsApp or contact number.
  • Your delivery address and city.
  • Product names, quantities and order details.
  • Payment transaction reference or payment confirmation information.
  • A prescription or other information required for legally permitted medicine supply, where applicable.
  • Other information you choose to share with us.

Please provide only information necessary for your enquiry or order. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive medical or financial information.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information shared with Kamal Sons Pharmacy may be used to:

  • Respond to product enquiries and customer questions.
  • Check product availability and confirm prices.
  • Process and verify order requests and payments.
  • Verify prescriptions where legally required.
  • Arrange delivery and communicate with delivery services.
  • Respond to complaints, cancellations and refund requests.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. WhatsApp Communication

Our website may contain links or buttons that open WhatsApp. When you use these links and send us a message, the information you share is handled through WhatsApp and its related services.

WhatsApp is a third-party service. Its own privacy policy and terms apply to your use of that platform.

We use messages and information you voluntarily send to respond to enquiries, communicate order details and coordinate customer service.

4. Orders and Delivery Information

To process an accepted order, we may use your name, phone number, delivery address and relevant order details.

Where necessary, limited delivery information may be shared with a courier company, inDrive, Yango or another delivery provider to arrange delivery.

These third-party providers handle information according to their own policies and terms. We share only the information reasonably necessary for the delivery arrangement.

5. Payments and Financial Information

Orders require advance online payment before dispatch, according to our Delivery, Payment & Return Policy.

Payment instructions are communicated directly by Kamal Sons Pharmacy. Payments may be processed through a bank, mobile wallet or other payment service selected for the transaction.

We do not ask customers to share banking passwords, PINs or OTPs with us. Customers should never disclose these confidential details to anyone.

Payment service providers may independently collect and process transaction information under their own privacy policies and terms. We may retain payment confirmation or transaction references needed to verify an order and handle related enquiries.

6. Website Hosting and Technical Information

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical information such as browser details, device information, IP address and server access data according to their own policies and terms.

Our website does not currently provide a customer account system or its own online database for storing website order submissions. Information sent directly through WhatsApp or other communication channels may remain available through those services and our business communications.

7. Cookies, Advertising and Google AdSense

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies if third-party services are introduced or enabled.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is activated, third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements and their own privacy policies.

Google may use information according to its advertising policies and privacy disclosures. Visitors can review Google's advertising and privacy controls to understand or manage available choices.

We will update this policy if advertising or analytics services are introduced or our data practices materially change.

8. Information Sharing

We do not sell customers' personal information.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with:

  • WhatsApp or other communication service providers used by customers.
  • Courier, inDrive, Yango or other delivery providers handling a delivery.
  • Banks, mobile wallets or payment providers involved in payment verification.
  • Website hosting and technical service providers.
  • Relevant authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

9. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable care when handling information shared with us. However, no website, online service or internet communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle enquiries, orders, payment records, complaints and applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically directed at children. Parents or guardians should make enquiries or orders on behalf of children and should avoid sharing unnecessary personal or medical information about them.

11. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have shared with Kamal Sons Pharmacy or to request correction of inaccurate contact details.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, regulatory, payment, safety or record-keeping purposes.

12. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may link to external services such as WhatsApp, Google and delivery providers. We do not control the privacy practices or content of these third-party services. Please review their own policies before using them.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, advertising arrangements or business services change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
