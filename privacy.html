Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

At Kamal Sons Pharmacy, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what happens to information when you visit our website or contact us to place an order.

1. Information We May Receive

When you contact us or place an order through WhatsApp, you may choose to share information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp or contact number
  • Your delivery address
  • Product names and order details
  • Any additional message you choose to send

Please share only the information necessary to process your enquiry or order. Do not send unnecessary sensitive medical or financial information.

2. WhatsApp Orders and Third-Party Services

Our website may provide links or buttons that open WhatsApp. When you use these links, information you send is handled through WhatsApp and its related services. WhatsApp is a third-party service and its own privacy policy and terms apply.

We use the information you voluntarily send to respond to enquiries, confirm orders, and coordinate delivery.

3. Website Data and Hosting

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process certain technical information, such as browser or device information and server access data, according to its own policies and terms.

Our website does not currently provide its own customer account system or online database for storing order submissions.

4. Payments

Our website does not collect online card or bank payment details. Product prices and order arrangements are confirmed directly with the customer.

5. Cookies and Advertising

We do not currently use our own advertising or analytics tracking cookies on this website. If we introduce analytics or advertising services in the future, this policy may be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell customers' personal information. Information you send may be used to handle your enquiry or order and may be processed by third-party services you choose to use, such as WhatsApp and website hosting providers.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable care when handling information shared with us. However, no website or internet communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general customers. Parents or guardians should contact us directly when making enquiries or orders on behalf of children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or services change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
