Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

Kamal Sons Pharmacy respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be received, used, shared and protected when you visit our website, contact us, submit a product enquiry or request an eligible order.

Important: Our website does not currently provide customer accounts or an online order submission database. Customers may contact us through WhatsApp or other available communication channels. Please share only the information necessary for your enquiry or accepted order.

1. Information We May Receive

When you contact us or request an eligible order, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your name.
  • Your WhatsApp or contact number.
  • Your delivery address and city.
  • Product names, quantities and order details.
  • Payment transaction reference or payment confirmation information.
  • Other information you choose to share that is necessary to handle your enquiry or order.

Please provide only information necessary for your enquiry or order. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive medical information, identity documents, banking passwords, PINs or OTPs.

Prescription medicines cannot be ordered through this website. If a prescription is legally required for a medicine, customers should visit the pharmacy and provide the required prescription there.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information voluntarily shared with Kamal Sons Pharmacy may be used to:

  • Respond to product enquiries and customer questions.
  • Check product availability and confirm prices.
  • Review and process eligible order requests.
  • Verify payment receipts and transaction references.
  • Arrange delivery and communicate relevant delivery details.
  • Respond to complaints, cancellations and refund requests.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. WhatsApp Communication

Our website may contain links or buttons that open WhatsApp. When you use these links and send us a message, the information you share is handled through WhatsApp and its related services.

WhatsApp is a third-party service. Its own privacy policy and terms apply to your use of that platform.

We use messages and information you voluntarily send to respond to enquiries, communicate order details and coordinate customer service.

Please avoid sending confidential banking information, passwords, PINs or OTPs through WhatsApp.

4. Orders and Delivery Information

To process an accepted eligible order, we may use your name, phone number, delivery address and relevant product or order details.

Where necessary, limited delivery information may be shared with a courier company, inDrive, Yango or another delivery provider to arrange delivery.

These third-party providers handle information according to their own policies and terms. We aim to share only the information reasonably necessary for the delivery arrangement.

5. Payments and Financial Information

Eligible orders require advance payment before dispatch, according to our Delivery, Payment & Return Policy.

Payment instructions are communicated directly by Kamal Sons Pharmacy. Payments may be made through a bank, mobile wallet or another payment service confirmed for the transaction.

We do not ask customers to share banking passwords, PINs or OTPs. Customers should never disclose these confidential details to anyone.

Payment service providers may independently collect and process transaction information under their own privacy policies and terms.

We may retain payment confirmation or transaction references reasonably needed to verify an order, handle customer enquiries or meet applicable record-keeping requirements.

6. Website Hosting and Technical Information

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical information such as browser details, device information, IP address and server access data according to their own policies and terms.

Our website does not currently provide a customer account system, payment gateway or its own online database for storing order submissions.

Information sent directly through WhatsApp or other communication channels may remain available through those services and our business communications.

7. Cookies, Advertising and Google AdSense

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies through third-party services or platform features.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is activated on our website, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements and their own privacy policies.

Google may process information according to its applicable privacy disclosures and advertising policies. Visitors can review Google's privacy and advertising controls to understand available choices.

We will update this Privacy Policy if advertising, analytics or other third-party services are introduced or our data practices materially change.

8. Information Sharing

We do not sell customers' personal information.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with:

  • WhatsApp or other communication service providers used by customers.
  • Courier, inDrive, Yango or other delivery providers handling a delivery.
  • Banks, mobile wallets or payment providers involved in payment verification.
  • Website hosting and technical service providers.
  • Relevant authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

We aim to limit information sharing to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

9. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable care when handling information shared with us. However, no website, online service or internet communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle enquiries, accepted orders, payment records, complaints and applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically directed at children. Parents or guardians should make enquiries or requests on behalf of children and should avoid sharing unnecessary personal or medical information about them.

11. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have shared directly with Kamal Sons Pharmacy or to request correction of inaccurate contact details.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, regulatory, payment, safety or record-keeping purposes.

Requests relating to information held by WhatsApp, Google, GitHub or other third-party services must generally be directed to the relevant service provider.

12. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may link to external services such as WhatsApp, Google, GitHub and delivery providers.

We do not control the privacy practices, security or content of these third-party services. Please review their own policies before using them.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, advertising arrangements or business services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
