About Kamal Sons Pharmacy

Welcome to Kamal Sons Pharmacy, a neighbourhood pharmacy based in Lahore, Pakistan. We aim to provide helpful customer service and information about selected healthcare, personal care and wellness products.

Our website allows customers to explore products and learn about our pharmacy services. WhatsApp online order requests are available only for products specifically verified and approved by the pharmacy for online sale.

Our Location

Ittefaq Town, Mansoorah, Multan Road, Lahore, Pakistan.

What We Offer

  • Selected healthcare and pharmacy products.
  • Personal care and baby care products.
  • Selected medical devices and wellness products.
  • Assistance with product availability, pack sizes and pricing.
  • WhatsApp enquiries and eligible product order requests.
  • Delivery arrangements subject to product eligibility and service availability.

Online Product Orders

Customers can browse available products on our website. Products specifically approved for online sale may display a WhatsApp Order Request button.

Customers can select an eligible product and send a request through WhatsApp. Our team will confirm product details, stock, pack size, final price and applicable delivery charges before accepting the order.

Products without online ordering approval are available for enquiry or pharmacy visit only. A WhatsApp enquiry does not automatically create or confirm an order.

Prescription Medicines – Pharmacy Visit Required

Prescription medicines cannot be ordered through this website. Customers requiring prescription medicines should visit Kamal Sons Pharmacy with a valid prescription where required. Supply is subject to applicable laws and the pharmacy's required verification.

Payment Information

Advance online payment is required only after the pharmacy confirms an eligible order and shares the payment instructions. Cash on Delivery (COD) is not available. Delivery and courier charges are separate from product prices and are paid by the customer.

Delivery Information

Delivery requests may be accepted in Lahore and other cities across Pakistan, subject to product eligibility, applicable requirements, service coverage and confirmation by the pharmacy.

Local delivery may be arranged through available delivery services. Orders for other cities may be sent through suitable courier services where permitted and available. Estimated delivery times are not guaranteed.

For complete details, please read our Delivery Policy.

Responsible Pharmacy Service

Medicines should be used responsibly and according to appropriate professional medical advice. Prescription medicines are not available for online ordering through this website.

Product information and educational guides on this website are provided for general information only. They are not a substitute for consultation, diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.
