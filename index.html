Kamal Sons Pharmacy – Lahore

Customers can send online order requests only for products specifically verified and approved by the pharmacy for online sale. The pharmacy confirms availability, prices, delivery arrangements and payment instructions before accepting an order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all listed products available?

Only products currently marked as available are displayed. Availability can change, so the pharmacy must confirm stock before accepting an order.

Which products can I order online?

Only products individually verified and approved by the pharmacy for online sale can use the WhatsApp order request option. Other products require a pharmacy visit.

Can I order prescription medicines online?

No. Prescription medicines cannot be ordered through this website. Please visit the pharmacy with a valid prescription where required.

Is advance payment required?

Yes. Advance online payment is required after the pharmacy confirms the eligible products, final amount and delivery charges. Cash on Delivery is not available.

Are delivery charges included?

No. Courier, inDrive, Yango or other delivery charges are paid separately by the customer and confirmed before payment.

Does this website provide medical advice?

No. The guides are for general educational information and do not replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.
