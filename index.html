Kamal Sons Pharmacy – Lahore

Kamal Sons Pharmacy is a neighbourhood pharmacy located in Ittefaq Town, Mansoorah, Multan Road, Lahore, Pakistan. Our aim is to provide customer assistance and information about selected healthcare and personal care products.

We believe that customers should be able to ask questions about product availability, pack sizes and prices before making a decision. Our website also provides educational guides on medicine safety and responsible medicine handling.

Medicines should be used according to appropriate medical advice. Prescription medicines are supplied only subject to applicable legal requirements and valid prescription verification. This website does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.
