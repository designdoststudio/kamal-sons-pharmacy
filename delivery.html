Delivery & Return Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

At Kamal Sons Pharmacy, we aim to make ordering healthcare products convenient for our customers. Please read this policy before placing an order through our website or WhatsApp.

1. Delivery Area

Our website is intended for customers in Lahore, Pakistan. Delivery availability depends on the customer's location and confirmation by the pharmacy.

Please contact us through WhatsApp to confirm whether delivery is available at your address.

2. Delivery Charges

Free home delivery may be available under certain arrangements. Any applicable delivery charges, including third-party courier or ride-hailing service charges, will be communicated to the customer before the order is confirmed.

Please confirm the final delivery charges through WhatsApp before placing your order.

3. Order Confirmation

Submitting an order request through the website does not automatically confirm the order.

The pharmacy will check product availability, prices, customer details and delivery arrangements. Your order will be considered confirmed only after the pharmacy communicates its acceptance.

4. Delivery Time

Delivery time may vary depending on the customer's location, product availability, traffic, weather and other circumstances.

Any estimated delivery time will be shared during order confirmation. Delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed.

5. Customer Responsibilities

Customers should provide a correct name, active phone number and complete delivery address.

Please keep your phone available so the pharmacy or delivery person can contact you if necessary.

6. Incorrect, Damaged or Missing Products

If you receive an incorrect, damaged or incomplete order, please contact Kamal Sons Pharmacy as soon as possible after delivery.

Where possible, keep the original packaging and provide details of the issue so that the pharmacy can review your complaint and advise on an appropriate solution.

7. Returns and Exchanges

Returns or exchanges are reviewed according to applicable laws, product type and safety requirements.

For medicines and healthcare products, returns may be restricted due to safety, storage and quality concerns. Products that have been opened, used, damaged after delivery or stored improperly may not be eligible for return, subject to applicable law.

Please contact the pharmacy before sending any product back. Do not send products without first receiving return instructions.

8. Order Cancellation

If you need to cancel an order, contact the pharmacy through WhatsApp as soon as possible. Cancellation may not be possible once an order has been dispatched or handed over for delivery.

9. Refunds

If a refund is approved, the pharmacy will discuss the applicable refund method and expected process directly with the customer. Refunds are subject to applicable laws and the circumstances of the order.
