Delivery, Payment & Return Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Kamal Sons Pharmacy aims to provide convenient access to healthcare products through order requests submitted via our website or WhatsApp. Please read the following policy before placing an order.

1. Delivery Areas

We may accept delivery requests from Lahore and other cities across Pakistan, subject to product availability, delivery arrangements and applicable legal requirements.

Delivery availability will be confirmed by the pharmacy after reviewing the customer's address and requested products.

2. Delivery Methods

Depending on the customer's location, delivery may be arranged through:

  • Local courier or delivery services.
  • inDrive or Yango, where available and suitable for the delivery.
  • Courier companies for deliveries to other cities in Pakistan.

The delivery method will be discussed with the customer before the order is confirmed. All deliveries are subject to service availability and applicable requirements for transporting medicines and healthcare products.

3. Delivery Charges

Delivery and courier charges are payable by the customer and are separate from the product prices.

Charges may vary depending on the delivery location, courier company, distance, package and service selected.

The applicable delivery charges will be communicated to the customer before order confirmation and payment.

4. Order Confirmation

Submitting an order request through our website or WhatsApp does not automatically confirm the order.

Our pharmacy will review product availability, prices, customer details, delivery address and applicable requirements.

The order will be confirmed only after the pharmacy communicates acceptance, provides the final payable amount and shares the payment instructions.

5. Advance Online Payment

All orders must be paid in advance through an online payment method confirmed by Kamal Sons Pharmacy before dispatch.

  • Payment is required before the order is dispatched.
  • Cash on delivery (COD) is not available under this policy.
  • The customer must verify the final product amount and delivery charges before making payment.
  • Dispatch will take place after payment has been received and verified by the pharmacy.

Payment instructions will be shared directly by the pharmacy through its official communication channel. Customers should not send payment to an unverified account or number.

Payment Safety: Never share your bank PIN, password, OTP or other confidential banking information with anyone. Keep your payment receipt or transaction reference until the order is delivered.

6. Delivery Time

Delivery time depends on the customer's location, product availability, courier service, traffic, weather and other circumstances.

An estimated delivery time will be communicated where possible. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and may change due to circumstances outside the pharmacy's control.

7. Customer Responsibilities

Customers must provide:

  • Correct name and active contact number.
  • Complete and accurate delivery address.
  • Any valid prescription or other information required for the requested medicine.
  • Availability to receive the parcel or coordinate with the delivery person.

Incorrect or incomplete delivery details may cause delays or additional delivery charges.

8. Prescription Medicines and Legal Requirements

Prescription medicines will only be supplied where permitted by applicable laws and regulations and after the required prescription verification.

Some medicines may require additional verification or may not be eligible for courier delivery. The pharmacy reserves the right to decline or cancel a request that does not meet applicable legal, safety or professional requirements.

9. Incorrect, Damaged or Missing Products

If you receive an incorrect, damaged or incomplete order, please contact Kamal Sons Pharmacy as soon as possible after delivery.

Where possible, keep the original packaging and provide clear details of the issue. The pharmacy will review the complaint and advise on an appropriate solution in accordance with applicable laws.

10. Returns and Exchanges

Returns and exchanges are handled according to applicable laws, product type, safety requirements and the circumstances of the complaint.

Due to medicine safety, storage and quality concerns, some medicines and healthcare products may not be eligible for return or exchange, except where required by applicable law or where an issue is attributable to the pharmacy.

Please contact the pharmacy before sending any product back. Do not return products without receiving instructions.

11. Order Cancellation

If you wish to cancel an order, contact the pharmacy as soon as possible.

Cancellation may not be possible after an order has been packed, dispatched or handed over to a courier or delivery person.

Any payment or refund request following cancellation will be reviewed according to the order status and applicable laws.

12. Refunds

If a refund is approved, the pharmacy will communicate the refund method and expected processing time to the customer.

Refunds will be handled according to applicable laws and the circumstances of the order. Any eligible refund of delivery charges will also be assessed according to the circumstances and applicable requirements.
