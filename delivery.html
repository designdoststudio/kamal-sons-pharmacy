Delivery, Payment & Return Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

Kamal Sons Pharmacy aims to provide convenient access to eligible healthcare and consumer products. Customers may submit order requests through our website or WhatsApp. Please read this policy before submitting a request.

Important: An online or WhatsApp order request is not an automatic order confirmation. Only products specifically marked as eligible for online ordering may be requested through the website. Prescription medicines are not available for online ordering through this website.

1. Delivery Areas

Delivery requests may be considered for Lahore and other cities in Pakistan, subject to product eligibility, availability, delivery arrangements, service coverage and applicable legal requirements.

The pharmacy will confirm whether delivery is available after reviewing the requested products and the customer's delivery address. Delivery to every area or city is not guaranteed.

2. Delivery Methods

Depending on the customer's location and the type of eligible products, delivery may be arranged through:

  • Local courier or delivery services.
  • inDrive or Yango, where available and suitable.
  • Courier companies for eligible deliveries to other cities in Pakistan.

The delivery method will be discussed with the customer before order confirmation. All deliveries are subject to service availability and applicable requirements for handling and transporting the relevant products.

3. Delivery Charges

Delivery and courier charges are payable by the customer and are separate from product prices.

Charges may vary depending on the delivery location, distance, package size and delivery service selected.

The applicable delivery charges will be communicated to the customer before order confirmation and payment.

4. Order Request and Confirmation

Submitting an order request through our website or WhatsApp does not automatically confirm an order or guarantee product availability.

Before accepting a request, the pharmacy will review:

  • Product availability and eligibility for online ordering.
  • Product details and final prices.
  • Customer contact details and delivery address.
  • Delivery availability and applicable charges.
  • Any legal, safety or verification requirements.

An order will be considered confirmed only after the pharmacy communicates its acceptance and provides the final payable amount and payment instructions.

5. Advance Online Payment

Payment must be made in advance through a payment method confirmed directly by Kamal Sons Pharmacy. Orders will not be dispatched until payment has been received and verified.

  • Cash on delivery (COD) is not available under this policy.
  • The customer should verify the final product amount and delivery charges before payment.
  • Payment should only be sent using the instructions provided by the pharmacy through its official communication channel.
  • The pharmacy will arrange dispatch after payment verification and completion of applicable checks.

Submitting an order request does not require the customer to make payment immediately. Please wait for the pharmacy to confirm the order and provide the final amount and payment instructions.

Payment Safety: Never share your bank PIN, password, OTP or other confidential banking information with anyone. Keep your payment receipt or transaction reference until the order is completed.

6. Delivery Time

Delivery time depends on the customer's location, product availability, courier service, traffic, weather and other circumstances.

An estimated delivery time may be communicated where possible. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and may change due to circumstances outside the pharmacy's control.

7. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for providing:

  • Correct name and active contact number.
  • Complete and accurate delivery address.
  • Any information or verification legally required for an eligible product.
  • Availability to receive the parcel or coordinate with the delivery person.

Incorrect or incomplete delivery details may cause delays, failed delivery or additional delivery charges.

8. Prescription Medicines and Legal Requirements

Prescription medicines cannot be ordered through this website. Customers requiring prescription medicines should visit the pharmacy with a valid prescription where required by law.

Any supply of prescription medicines will be subject to applicable laws, prescription verification and professional requirements.

Some medicines and healthcare products may require additional checks or may not be suitable for courier delivery. The pharmacy reserves the right to decline a request that does not meet applicable legal, safety or professional requirements.

9. Incorrect, Damaged or Missing Products

If you receive an incorrect, damaged or incomplete order, please contact Kamal Sons Pharmacy as soon as reasonably possible after delivery.

Where possible, keep the original packaging and provide clear details of the issue. Photographs may help the pharmacy review the complaint.

The pharmacy will review the matter and communicate an appropriate solution in accordance with applicable laws and the circumstances of the order.

10. Returns and Exchanges

Returns and exchanges are handled according to applicable laws, product type, safety requirements and the circumstances of the complaint.

Due to medicine safety, storage and quality concerns, some medicines and healthcare products may not be suitable for return or exchange. This does not affect any rights or remedies available to customers under applicable law.

If you receive an incorrect, damaged, defective or otherwise problematic product, please contact the pharmacy for review and instructions before sending anything back.

Do not return products without first contacting the pharmacy and receiving return instructions.

11. Order Cancellation

If you wish to cancel an order, contact the pharmacy as soon as possible.

Cancellation may not be possible after an order has been packed, dispatched or handed over to a courier or delivery person.

Any payment or refund request following cancellation will be reviewed according to the order status, applicable laws and the circumstances of the request.

12. Refunds

If a refund is approved, the pharmacy will communicate the refund method and expected processing details to the customer.

Refund eligibility will be assessed according to applicable laws, the nature of the complaint and the circumstances of the order.

Any eligible refund of delivery charges will also be assessed according to the circumstances and applicable requirements.
