Product Enquiries and Orders

For product availability, pack sizes, current prices and order requests, please contact us through WhatsApp. You can also send an order request using the buttons provided on our product listings.

Our team will review your request and confirm stock availability, final product prices and applicable delivery charges before accepting the order.

Payment Information

Advance online payment is required. Payment instructions are shared after the pharmacy confirms the order details.

Cash on delivery (COD) is not available. Please do not send payment before receiving confirmation and official payment instructions from the pharmacy.

Delivery Information

Delivery requests are accepted for Lahore and other cities across Pakistan, subject to delivery coverage and availability.

Delivery and courier charges are separate from product prices and are paid by the customer. Orders are dispatched after payment has been received and verified by the pharmacy.

For more information, please visit our Delivery Information page.

Customer Support

Our team aims to respond to enquiries as soon as possible during available business hours. Response and delivery times may vary depending on order details and service availability.

Important Medical Notice

This website provides product information and a way to send order requests. It does not provide emergency medical services, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice. Prescription medicines are supplied only where legally permitted and after applicable prescription verification.
