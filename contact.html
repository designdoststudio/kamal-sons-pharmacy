Product Enquiries and Order Requests

For product availability, pack sizes, current prices and order enquiries, please contact us through WhatsApp. You can also submit an order request using the buttons provided on eligible product listings.

Our team will review your request and confirm product availability, final prices and applicable delivery charges before accepting an order.

Submitting a WhatsApp message or website order request does not automatically confirm an order.

Payment Information

Advance online payment is required for accepted orders. Payment instructions are shared after the pharmacy confirms the order details and final payable amount.

Cash on delivery (COD) is not available. Please do not send payment before receiving confirmation and official payment instructions from the pharmacy.

Orders are dispatched after payment has been received and verified.

Delivery Information

Delivery requests may be considered for Lahore and other cities across Pakistan, subject to product eligibility, delivery coverage and availability.

Delivery and courier charges are separate from product prices and are payable by the customer. Orders are dispatched after payment verification and completion of applicable checks.

For more information, please visit our Delivery, Payment & Return Policy page.

Customer Support

Our team aims to respond to enquiries as soon as possible during available business hours. Response and delivery times may vary depending on order details and service availability.

Important Medical Notice

Prescription medicines cannot be ordered through this website. Customers requiring prescription medicines should visit the pharmacy with a valid prescription where required by law.

This website provides general product information and a way to submit enquiries or eligible order requests. It does not provide emergency medical services, medical diagnosis or treatment.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice. Do not start, stop or change any medicine solely on the basis of information displayed on this website.
